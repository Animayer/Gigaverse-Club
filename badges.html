Badges

Twelve sample achievements for the demo vault. Each badge stacks bronze, silver, and gold. Earned tiers stay lit. Locked tiers keep a progress bar. Faction Wars points can unlock badges. Points still come from activity, not wallet size. The holder board is a different list: it sorts sample wallets by how many pieces they hold.

Onchain badges possible later. This page does not mint, sign, or read a contract.
